About

Kato.8 is built around a simple principle: don’t confuse ambition with scope. We aim for games that look distinct, feel great to play, and can be shipped reliably.

What we optimize for

  • Clarity: ownership, decisions, milestones
  • Craft: strong identity and “feel”
  • Momentum: frequent shippable increments
  • Discipline: deliberate feature selection
